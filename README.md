# Qimma
Qimma is an educational social platform connecting students and teachers through study resources, live classes, interactive communities, exam preparation, and smart productivity tools.


# Qimma 🎓
### Reach Your Peak.

Qimma is an all-in-one educational and social platform built for high school students and teachers. It combines learning resources, social networking, live classes, and productivity tools in one connected learning community.

Our goal is to make education more accessible, interactive, and collaborative by bringing students and teachers together in one place.

## ✨ Core Features

### 📚 1. Educational Resources
- Study summaries, notes, and revision sheets.
- Subject-based libraries organized by grade and curriculum.
- Shared educational materials and student contributions.
- Practice questions, quizzes, and mock exams.
- Search and filter resources by subject, teacher, or topic.
- Save favorite materials for quick access.

### 👨‍🏫 2. Teachers Hub
- A unified directory bringing teachers from different subjects together.
- Teacher profiles with biographies, subjects, and educational content.
- Follow teachers to receive updates about new lessons and resources.
- Teacher ratings and student reviews.
- Verified teacher profiles to help students identify authentic accounts.
- Lesson schedules and announcements.

### 🔴 3. Live Streaming & Online Classes
- Live educational sessions hosted by teachers.
- Integration with external streaming platforms such as YouTube Live and other supported services.
- A central hub for discovering upcoming and ongoing live classes.
- Live chat, student questions, and interactive discussions.
- Scheduled sessions with reminders and calendar integration.
- Recordings and replays of classes when available and authorized.
- Live Q&A sessions and revision marathons before exams.

### 🌐 4. Educational Social Network
- Student and teacher profiles.
- Educational posts, comments, likes, and sharing.
- Follow classmates, teachers, and educational communities.
- Subject-specific communities and study groups.
- Ask questions and receive answers from students and teachers.
- Direct messaging with privacy and safety controls.
- Share achievements, study milestones, and useful resources.

### ⏱️ 5. Smart Study Tools
- Focus timer with customizable study sessions.
- Daily and weekly study planners.
- Personal goals and task management.
- Study streaks and progress tracking.
- Break reminders and productivity statistics.
- Personal dashboard for upcoming classes, tasks, and saved resources.

### 🧠 6. Exams & Assessment
- Interactive quizzes with instant feedback.
- Practice exams organized by subject and difficulty.
- Explanations for answers and common mistakes.
- Personal performance reports to identify weak areas.
- Exam countdowns and revision plans.
- Leaderboards for optional, friendly academic competition.

### 🤖 7. AI Learning Assistant
- Explain difficult concepts in simple language.
- Summarize educational material.
- Generate practice questions from study notes.
- Help students create personalized revision plans.
- Recommend resources based on learning needs.
- Provide hints and step-by-step guidance rather than just answers.

### 🔔 8. Smart Notifications
- Alerts for upcoming live classes and exams.
- Updates from followed teachers and communities.
- Notifications for replies, comments, and messages.
- Personalized reminders based on study goals.
- Customizable notification preferences.

### 🛡️ 9. Trust, Safety & Accessibility
- Report inappropriate content and suspicious accounts.
- Moderation tools for teachers and community administrators.
- Verified teacher identities where verification is available.
- Privacy controls for profiles and messages.
- Responsive design for mobile phones, tablets, and computers.
- Accessible and easy-to-use interfaces.

## 🚀 Future Vision

Qimma aims to become a complete digital learning ecosystem where students can discover teachers, attend live lessons, access study resources, collaborate with classmates, prepare for exams, and track their academic progress.

The platform will continue to evolve through student feedback, teacher participation, and new educational technologies.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower every student with the resources, community, and tools they need to learn effectively, build confidence, and achieve their full potential.

**Qimma — Learn Together. Rise Together. Reach Your Peak.**
